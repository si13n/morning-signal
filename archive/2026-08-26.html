Top Signal Priority 1/10

Junie Local: run JetBrains’ coding agent entirely on-device

JetBrains released Junie Local: a one-command, on‑device agent that ships a tuned Qwen3.6-27B model (≈20GB) and runs on M5 Macs (64GB), keeping code, prompts, and diffs local and unmetered. (Aug 24–25, 2026).

Why it matters

Enables private, offline agent-driven refactors, test-generation, and coverage work without cloud tokens — useful for teams with NDAs and for iterating expensive QA tasks locally.
