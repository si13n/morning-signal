Test infrastructure / Containerized test runners2/10

Kubernetes: shift to cgroup v2 — resource semantics and compatibility guidance

Kubernetes published guidance on moving clusters to cgroup v2, detailing kernel requirements, task freezer behavior, CPU/memory accounting differences, and migration caveats that affect scheduling and observability.

Why it matters

Teams running containerized test runners, load tests or agent sandboxes must validate test harness behavior and performance baselines under cgroup v2 to avoid misleading test regressions.
