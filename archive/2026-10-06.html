What is Agent Experience (AX)? — measuring agent correctness with AX

Microsoft’s AX guidance describes measuring how well coding agents use project tech, choose APIs, and complete tasks — including scenario-based runs and task-level metrics for agent-tool interactions. ([developer.microsoft.com](https://developer.microsoft.com/blog/tag/agent-experience/?utm_source=openai))

Why it matters

AX gives QA teams concrete measurement targets (tooling compatibility, task success rates) for validating agent-driven test creation and automation. ([developer.microsoft.com](https://developer.microsoft.com/blog/tag/agent-experience/?utm_source=openai))

Pizza Bot: an open-source inbox for background AI agents

AWS released Pizza Bot, a self-hosted inbox model for long-running background agents that preserves provenance, approval boundaries, and scheduled runs — designed for asynchronous agent work and inspection. ([aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/opensource/introducing-pizza-bot-an-open-source-inbox-for-ai-agents-that-work-in-the-background/))

Why it matters

Makes background agent outputs auditable and reviewable — important for QA workflows, human-in-the-loop approvals, and reproducible failure triage of agent-driven tests. ([aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/opensource/introducing-pizza-bot-an-open-source-inbox-for-ai-agents-that-work-in-the-background/))
